Welcome to the Future of Data Integration

In today's data-driven world, the ability to process information quickly and intelligently is paramount. GSmart was built to bridge the gap between complex data sources and insightful business analytics. Our desktop application provides a robust, user-friendly interface to automate your data workflows, allowing you to focus on what truly matters: making informed decisions.

Core Features

Flexible Data Sources

Easily connect to IoT platforms like ThingsBoard or relational databases (e.g., PostgreSQL) to collect telemetry and records.

Intelligent Rule Engine

Create custom alert and alarm rules. Define conditions and thresholds to monitor metrics and get notified about critical events or business insights.

BI Tool Integration

Send processed and transformed data directly to dashboards in Power BI or to Eventstreams in Microsoft Fabric, ready for analysis.

Proactive Notifications

Receive real-time alerts via MQTT or Telegram when a rule is triggered, enabling a swift response to anomalies or important events.

User Management

Control application access with a login and user management system, defining Administrator and Operator roles with different permissions.

Centralized Monitoring

View and manage all your active pipelines from a monitoring center, tracking the status and uptime of each task.
